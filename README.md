Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing 4 years of Superstore retail sales data from 2014-2017.
The goal is to help leadership track KPIs, identify growth drivers, and make data-driven decisions across Region, Category, and State. Da

Tools Used

Power BI, DAX Data Modeling, Data Visualization Storytelling

Dataset 

Source: Superstore US Sample Dataset 

Records: 5,099 Orders 

Time Period: Jan 2014 - Dec 2017 

Geography: 4 Regions, 49 States across USA  

Categories: Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies  

Key Fields: Order Date, Region, State, Category, Sales, Profit, Quantity, Discount

Business Questions Answered

1. Which region generates the most revenue?
  West leads with $724K. South is weakest at $466K.

2. Which categories are driving growth?
   Technology $836K is top. Furniture has revenue but low margins.
   
3. What trends can decision-makers learn from?
   Strong YoY growth from 2015-2017.
   
4. Which states generate the most profit?
   CA, NY, TX, WA are top profit states.

Key Features

4 KPI Cards: Total Revenue $2.30M, Total Profit $286.4K, Total Orders 5,099, Profit Margin 12%.

3 Interactive Slicers: Order Date, Region, Category.

Visuals: 

Sales by Category - Donut Chart

Yearly Sales Trend - Line Chart 

Sales by Region - Bar Chart with Drill-down to State

Profit by State - Filled Map


Key Insights

1. West + East are the strongest regions. South needs improvement.
   
2. Technology is most profitable. Furniture needs pricing review.
   
3. Profit is concentrated in CA, NY, TX, WA.
   
4. Business scaled successfully post-2015 with sharp growth.


Recommendations
1. Invest in West Region, Technology, and Top 5 Profit States.
   
2. Improve South Region performance and Furniture margins.
